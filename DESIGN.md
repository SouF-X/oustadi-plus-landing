# Oustadi Plus — De la séance à la suite

## Direction
A center owner opens the site on a phone between two busy teaching sessions. The page should explain the product quickly in bright daylight and reward exploration. The user explicitly prefers the original landing page’s white hero, expressive three-line headline and classroom-to-phone composition. Restore that direction: a mint teaching board, a purple-accented student phone, a Darija sticker and the student’s moment of understanding. The hero illustrates one familiar lesson moving from the center into the student’s evening; an interactive publication/access journey follows below. This user preference supersedes the initial purple-hero proposal.

## Tokens
Mulberry #68234F, ink #291E2B, lilac #F1E9F4, apricot #FFCCA9, white #FFFFFF. Express CSS tokens in OKLCH matching these established colors. Bricolage Grotesque for display; Manrope for French body; Noto Sans Arabic for Darija. These are existing licensed brand fonts. Max display 5.5rem, tracking no tighter than -0.04em. Controls 44px minimum, corners 12–16px except device frames and pills.

## Composition
Quiet navigation, white hero with the original “La séance finit. Le déclic continue.” rhythm and a classroom-to-phone scene, then a lilac transition strip with a permanent Darija line. The decorative teaching scene uses real mathematical geometry. A three-role interactive course journey follows. Follow with one substantial role explorer, a student learning section with working format examples, provider identity personalization, an honest prototype status note and FAQ, then a pilot brief. Vary section proportions and spacing. Illustrative content is marked as example data.

## Identity
Use the approved education mark already present in the prototype as the working identity. A separate unapproved logo proposal may explore continuity through a folded lesson/bookmark, without silently replacing the app's assets. No trademark or audience recognition claims.

## Behavior
Publication and access decisions update the marketing demo's student state. Quiz answers produce a real result; document and offline examples are explicitly simulations. Provider name and accent customization update the student preview. French/Darija switches translate all controls and preserve interaction state. Native dialog, real validation, useful downloadable brief, no submissions to third parties. Avoid gating content on entrance animations.

## Motion and editing

The hero tells one 3.81-second story: the teacher draws the curve and its minimum, a lesson emerges from that point and follows a curved path into the phone, then the student’s realization and checkmark appear. It plays once when the scene is visible, with an explicitly requested replay. Native Web Animations API and SVG stroke drawing require no runtime dependency. The transfer is measured from real element positions after local fonts load; resize or language changes finish safely to the static composition. Hidden tabs pause; reduced motion and script failure keep the complete scene readable. Publication/access tabs have 220–300ms continuity transitions, progress completion takes 440ms, and quiz feedback takes 180ms. No looping decoration or repeated section entrances.

Remove repeated explanations from the scene and the page: no audience eyebrow, duplicate hero note, floating caption, scene footer, section kickers or generic FAQ/closing introductions. Retain useful product labels, the demo-data label, payment/access distinction, prototype status, proposed personalization and the local-only form explanation.

## Page chrome and demo form

A thin lilac divider separates the header from the hero. The page scrollbar uses a slim mulberry thumb on a pale lilac track, with no arrow buttons; platform/high-contrast fallbacks remain native. Open dialogs lock background scrolling. Their visible scrollbar is suppressed while native touch, wheel and keyboard scrolling remain available when needed.

The pilot brief uses a lilac introduction beside a practical form on wide screens and a compact single-column layout on smaller screens. A short-height variant removes the generic introductory sentence so the primary controls and privacy statement fit the window. Center/teacher selection uses native radio inputs with full-size labeled surfaces. Required fields, the local text download, Escape and restored focus are preserved. French and Darija remain complete. No sign-in or data submission is added.
