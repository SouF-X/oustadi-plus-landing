## Platform

## Audience and job
Moroccan tutoring centers are the primary buyers; independent teachers are a supported second audience. The page explains how physical Swaye3 gains a continuous digital learning space. Students use the learning app; staff and teachers control access and content. The visitor should understand the complete journey and prepare a pilot conversation.

## Confirmed business
Providers recruit their students. Oustadi Plus supplies infrastructure, not a public marketplace. Teacher-owned videos, documents and quizzes are organized into courses. Center reception handles student access. Student payment is external and separate from authorization. One shared application can contain a provider's own space. No paying customers, adoption statistics or launch date have been confirmed.

## Personality and references
Direct, generous, confident. Preserve the purple identity already adopted by the prototype. Carry the clarity of a course syllabus and the warmth of a familiar Moroccan teaching provider. Avoid generic startup metrics, unverifiable promises, decorative AI motifs and marketplace discovery claims.

## Conversion and proof
Primary: experience a short interactive publication-to-learning journey. Secondary: download a pilot brief with the provider's own details. The form explicitly saves on the visitor's device and sends nothing. No fabricated contact details or sales backend. Source repository is linked as a prototype, without exposing its demonstration personal data in marketing.

## Languages and accessibility
French and plain Moroccan Darija, fully translated with RTL layout. Keyboard operation, visible focus, touch targets, reduced motion, no color-only statuses, tested narrow layouts. No parent account or unsupported platform badge.
